高锰酸钾制氧气实验
Potassium Permanganate Oxygen Production

高锰酸钾 (KMnO₄) / Potassium Permanganate

实验装置问题 / Setup Question

为什么试管要略向下倾斜？
Why should the test tube be slightly tilted downward?

使气体流动更顺畅 / To allow better gas flow
防止水倒流 / To prevent water from entering
防止冷凝水回流使试管炸裂 / To prevent condensation from cracking the tube
加快反应速度 / To make the reaction faster
正确答案：试管口略向下倾斜是为了防止加热时产生的水蒸气在试管口冷凝后倒流，导致试管炸裂。
Correct answer: The test tube is tilted downward to prevent water vapor from condensing and flowing back into the hot tube, which could cause it to crack.

加热过程问题 / Heating Question

加热高锰酸钾的主要目的是什么？
What is the main purpose of heating potassium permanganate?

提纯化合物 / To purify the compound
改变其颜色 / To change its color
使其分解产生氧气 / To decompose it into oxygen and other products
使其溶于水 / To make it dissolve in water
正确答案：加热高锰酸钾使其分解，产生氧气、锰酸钾和二氧化锰。
Correct answer: Heating causes potassium permanganate to decompose into oxygen gas, potassium manganate, and manganese dioxide.

气体收集问题 / Collection Question

为什么用排水法收集氧气？
Why is oxygen collected by downward displacement of water?

因为氧气比水轻 / Because oxygen is lighter than water
因为氧气微溶于水 / Because oxygen is slightly soluble in water
因为氧气不易溶于水 / Because oxygen is not very soluble in water
因为水会与氧气反应 / Because water reacts with oxygen
正确答案：氧气在水中溶解度较低(室温下每升水约溶解30cm³氧气)，适合用排水法收集。
Correct answer: Oxygen is collected this way because it's only slightly soluble in water (about 30 cm³ per liter at room temperature).

气体检验问题 / Testing Question

如何检验收集到的气体是氧气？
How would you confirm the collected gas is oxygen?

用带火星的木条复燃 / Using a glowing splint that reignites
观察其颜色 / By observing its color
测量其温度 / By measuring its temperature
闻其气味 / By checking its smell
正确答案：氧气能支持燃烧，可使带火星的木条复燃。
Correct answer: Oxygen supports combustion, so it will cause a glowing splint to burst into flames when inserted into the gas.

实验要点 / Key Points of the Experiment:

  • 化学方程式 / Chemical Formula: 2KMnO₄ → K₂MnO₄ + MnO₂ + O₂↑
  • 实验装置 / Apparatus: 试管、酒精灯、导管、水槽、集气瓶 / Test tube, Bunsen burner, delivery tube, water trough, gas collection jar
  • 方法 / Method: 加热试管中的高锰酸钾，用排水法收集氧气 / Heat potassium permanganate in a test tube and collect oxygen by downward displacement of water
  • 注意事项 / Precautions:
    • 试管口略向下倾斜防止水倒流 / Tilt the test tube downward to prevent water backflow
    • 先移出导管再熄灭酒精灯 / Remove delivery tube before turning off heat
    • 保持通风良好，因为氧气助燃 / Use proper ventilation as oxygen supports combustion
    • 加热时戴护目镜 / Wear safety goggles when heating
    • 高锰酸钾有腐蚀性，避免直接接触 / Potassium permanganate is corrosive - avoid direct contact
